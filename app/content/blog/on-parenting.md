A couple of wild things I've discovered:
- Fathers spend more than **3x** time with their kids than what they did in 1965
- Formula use is increasing with time to **22.6% of mothers** supplementing with formula
- **74% of mother**s are in the paid labor force
- We're at a **50-year** low in births
- The **first child is coming at age 27**, compared to 23 in 1965
